Privacy Policy for Physical DMH

Effective Date: October 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Physical DMH mobile application (collectively, the "Application") operated by LNM ("Service Provider", "we", "our", or "us").

1. Camera Usage & Real-Time Motion Processing

Strict Privacy Commitment: Physical DMH uses your device's camera exclusively for real-time exercise motion tracking and automatic repetition counting using local Machine Learning algorithms (Google ML Kit).

  • On-Device Processing: All camera video streams and pose-detection analysis occur entirely offline and locally on your mobile device.

The Application collects anonymous operational and performance data when downloaded and used, including:

  • Your device's Internet Protocol (IP) address.
  • Operating system version and device model information.
